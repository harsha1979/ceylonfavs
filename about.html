Who we are

Ceylon Favorites is an online store for authentic Sri Lankan food and products. We bring a curated selection of groceries, spices, teas, beverages, snacks, frozen foods and specialty items from trusted Sri Lankan brands and suppliers — straight to your door.

Our mission

Whether you're reconnecting with the flavours you grew up with, or exploring Sri Lankan cuisine for the very first time, we want it to be easy. That means quality products, friendly service and convenient shopping — with shipping, local delivery and pickup options to suit you.

🇱🇰 From hill-country tea to kithul treacle, Maliban biscuits to Fab frozen short eats — if it's a Sri Lankan favourite, we want it on our shelves.
