Return Policy

Something not right? Here's how returns and refunds work.

Overview Shipping Policy Return Policy
✉️

Email us

Send your order number, the item(s) and the reason to support@ceylonfavs.com within 20 days.

📦

Ship it back

Once approved, we'll send return instructions. Return shipping is paid by the customer.

💳

Get refunded

Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Eligibility

  • Request a return within 20 days of receiving your order.
  • Items must be unused, unopened and in their original packaging — in the same condition you received them.
  • A receipt or proof of purchase is required.
  • Returns sent without prior approval may be rejected.

Refunds

After we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to let you know whether it's approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

Late or missing refunds

Please check with your bank first. If you still need help, email support@ceylonfavs.com.

Undeliverable or returned packages

If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect address, refused delivery, failed delivery attempts or other reasons outside our control, we'll issue a refund once it's back at our facility. Carrier charges (such as UPS) are non-refundable and will be deducted.

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout — you're responsible for providing accurate delivery information.

Need help?

Our team is happy to help with orders, shipping options and frozen deliveries.
