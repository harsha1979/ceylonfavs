Shipping Policy

We ship to all 50 U.S. states, with local delivery and store pickup options too.

Overview Shipping Policy Return Policy

Last updated: 6/7/2026

Shipping coverage

We ship to all 50 U.S. states.

Rates & local delivery

  • Weekend local delivery within 50 miles is a flat $10 — for both dry and frozen items.
  • Shipping costs are calculated automatically at checkout based on weight, size and delivery location. You'll see the final rate before you pay.
  • Orders beyond 50 miles — including within Texas — are priced at checkout using available UPS options.
  • Frozen items shipped more than 35 miles need a separate insulated cooling box. If local delivery isn't available for your distance, choose a UPS option at checkout.

Frozen products

  • Local pickup & local delivery: dry and frozen items can be in the same order.
  • UPS shipping: frozen items must be ordered separately from dry items so we can use the correct insulated packaging and service.
  • Frozen UPS orders use 1–2 business day service where possible and usually ship Monday–Wednesday (excluding holidays) so packages don't sit in a UPS facility over the weekend.
  • We pack with insulated materials plus gel packs and/or dry ice, depending on order size.

Not sure which option to pick? Contact us before ordering and we'll help.
